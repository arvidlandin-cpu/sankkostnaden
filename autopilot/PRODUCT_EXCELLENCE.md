# Sänk Kostnaden — product excellence north star

Owner mandate, 2026-10-08: **Do not merely polish an affiliate website. Build the best possible Swedish consumer experience for understanding, reducing and switching recurring household costs.** "Best" is an ambition to test against demanding criteria and competitors, never a claim to publish without proof. The business objective remains long-term **approved affiliate revenue per genuinely relevant visitor** subject to correct, transparent user outcomes.

This document is binding input to Scout, Analyst, Planner, Operator, Red Team and Verifier alongside `PLAYBOOK.md`, `STRATEGY_AND_EVIDENCE_STANDARD.md`, competitor intelligence, policy and state. It is not permission to skip QA, fabricate proof or start overlapping experiments.

## Stop thinking in individual fixes — think in the complete product

The product is the user's entire journey, not just its visible cards or a higher Google position:
1. **Find us.** Map the real problems consumers search for. Win opportunities where original, useful answers and tools can be shared, cited, discovered and ranked. Develop relevant non-SEO distribution with real audience fit.
2. **Understand the value in seconds.** On the first mobile screen, answer "what do I get, why should I trust it, what do I do next?" without unexplained jargon, excessive decoration, claims we cannot support or six competing buttons.
3. **Serve a confused beginner.** A genuinely useful 0–2-question path for "jag betalar nog för mycket men vet inte vad jag har"; do not require kWh, Mbps, a policy document or contract terms to get any value.
4. **Also serve detail-oriented people.** Optional exact input, accurate first-year/total-cost calculation, assumptions and clear limitations. Never pass estimated/illustrative results off as provider quotes.
5. **Help reach a decision.** Make provider coverage, recognizable brands, real differentiators, partner type, eligibility, pricing/fee uncertainty, disclosure and next action understandable. Sort for user relevance and trust; do not use commission as a hidden ranking input.
6. **Get the action completed.** A coherent, minimally interrupted path to a genuine relevant quote, partner comparison or provider. Measure actual qualified tagged clicks and approved commission, and diagnose drops between steps.
7. **Earn long-term trust.** Source/freshness for prices and claims, accessible privacy-first UI, clear how-it-works and methodology, mobile speed, real editorial/automation disclosure, no fake ratings or social proof.
8. **Learn and compound.** Research competitors and changing markets every day, log evidence and hypotheses, perform high-quality bounded releases, objectively review, reject weak ideas, then reuse only validated patterns across categories.

## Competitive benchmark scorecard — audit the whole product

Maintain a dated **category-specific** benchmark across leading comparable services, suppliers, official resources and genuinely innovative out-of-category patterns. Do not claim market leadership from a subjective score. For each category assess: (a) search demand and visibility, (b) first-screen clarity, (c) friction/time to first useful answer, (d) accuracy and live-data limitations, (e) usefulness/independence of comparison, (f) partner visibility and fair choice, (g) CTA progression and approved-revenue path, (h) mobile/a11y/performance, (i) verifiable trust, (j) differentiation and shareability. Each dimension must show:
- Competitor observation with link and date (what can actually be seen).
- Our current observed behavior and technical/data capabilities.
- User job; hypothesis why the difference might matter.
- Gap: **inferior / equivalent / meaningfully differentiated / UNKNOWN**; no invented scores, visits, conversion metrics or partner earnings.
- Specific high-value next step, required data/tool/source and test/QA criterion.

Compare within categories first; note where a competitor has proprietary live provider feeds, customer reviews, address coverage or quote systems we do not have. Design an *original, truthful* alternative rather than pretending we have identical capability.

## The product architecture to work toward

**One recognizable system with distinct jobs:** homepage = fast household-cost overview and discovery; category pages = simple relevant decision + visible real providers; targeted guides = answer the exact search question first, then a helpful action; calculators = transparent, reusable, easy to share; partner handoff = relevance + clear value + genuine sponsored disclosure; /app/ = one low-friction overview and optional detailed route. Preserve SEO intent and canonical strategy.

Partner-brand recognition matters, but enlarging logos alone does not make a site world-class. Build a coherent hierarchy that explains who each partner is, who it may suit, verified offer/contract differences where available and why the user is being sent onward. Be honest when there is no live price feed; avoid duplicate direct/compare/pick routes that simply shuffle static priority.

Before implementing any design change, define **exactly** why each heading, illustration, color emphasis, field, partner-card position, logo size, CTA and disclosure exists in that user journey. Consider the simplest possible full-page alternative, not only a small edit to a compromised layout. Keep accessible hit areas and mobile-first QA at 360/390/430px.

## Execution standard: ambitious direction, evidence-based increments

- **Strategic blueprint first:** always view the page in relation to adjacent pages and conversion sequence. Research 2+ applicable competitor examples when feasible and two alternative designs, including a fundamental rethink of the journey rather than only polishing existing blocks.
- **Build in coherent slices:** prioritize highest user+business leverage that can safely ship now; do not hold the entire roadmap hostage to a 7-day metadata test or a tiny organic sample. Scoped independent initiatives may proceed within the concurrent policy limits. Changes to the same category/funnel require sequencing.
- **Demand proof appropriate to the question:** objective build, a11y, privacy, integrity, display and calculator arithmetic can be tested immediately; true user preference, organic acquisition uplift and approved affiliate revenue need actual samples/time. Record UNKNOWN when unproved.
- **Investment discipline:** rank by addressable intent, original usefulness, differentiation, likelihood of reaching real users, qualified downstream conversion, cost/complexity and evidence. Don't spend weeks perfecting low-traffic cards while discovery is the binding problem.
- **Growth loops beyond passive SEO:** seek high-quality original utilities, link-worthy consumer research, relevant placements, legitimate partnerships and low-friction distribution. No spam, purchased links, unverifiable claims or paid services without owner permission.
- **Same high bar for every category:** don't roll out a successful-looking electricity visual mechanically to insurance, mobile, broadband and finance; validate information needs, partners, compliance and user action per context.
- **Release must improve the whole experience:** when shipping a change, log what the visitor sees before and after, the user benefit, real next action, what is measured, regression checklist and rollback. If an entire flow is structurally inferior to a more direct alternative, propose redesign rather than endless local patching.
- **Challenge your own work:** relentlessly reconsider previous AI decisions when market evidence, user screenshots or real behavior expose weak assumptions. Retire design patterns that fail high product standards.

## Review rhythm

**Daily:** operations checks and public market/competitor discovery, identify bottleneck and one or two independent executable priorities; update source-backed opportunity map without duplicate busywork.

**Weekly:** revisit the full competitive benchmark, product journey, most important user jobs, revenue bottleneck, new distribution candidates, missing enabling data/integrations and evidence behind each next move. Re-rank across ALL categories, not merely the category touched most recently.

**After each ship:** verify production checks and observe qualified usage. When samples mature, approved revenue per relevant user is the decisive commercial measure, with usefulness and trust non-negotiable.

The deliverable is **a distinctly excellent, coherent, useful consumer service** that can attract and convert qualified visitors; it is not a tall backlog of tiny improvements or AI-reported activity.
