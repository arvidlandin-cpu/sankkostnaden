## Fully reasoned 13-field decision

1. **Audience/job.** A novice household arrives by organic search or a relevant personal recommendation, may not know their bill, wants an easy start or a direct category. On a 390px phone, answer “Vad gör jag nu?” rather than asking them to interpret a row of repeated competing CTAs.
2. **Observed benchmarks (as at 2026-10-13, first-party sites):** [Bredbandsval.se](https://www.bredbandsval.se/) opens with one address input and its *Sök* action, with 21 reported suppliers and actual address-specific offers; we cannot reproduce that coverage without an arrangement/data. [Elskling](https://www.elskling.se/jamfor) has a single primary action *Se mina bästa priser*, but demands housing/postcode and contact fields in its visible form; we do not have those live quotations. These are visible web observations, not measured competitor conversion rates. Lyst/The Zebra/KAYAK remain conceptual UX inspiration, not benchmark performance proof.
3. **Observation versus hypothesis.** Our genuine 390px first-fold after P0M has two separate filled-blue actions (header **Börja jämföra** and hero **Starta Kostnadskollen**), an additional hero text action **Välj en kostnad direkt**, four home explorer choices and a five-category fixed bottom dock. Source-confirmed links in `pages/index.tsx` + `HomeHero.tsx`; screenshot confirmed at a normal 390 CSS-pixel viewport. Hypothesis: removing *duplicate* top actions at mobile widths reduces cognitive competition and reveals the primary start faster, without preventing direct category access. No visitor-outcome proof yet.
4. **Internal evidence/gaps.** The screenshot `first-fold-p0m-home-390.png` captured after the latest P0M photography build illustrates competing blue actions and links. `styles/Home.module.css` shows `.navCta` visible on mobile, `styles/HomeHero.module.css` `.secondary` visible. `MobileQuickBar` contains five direct category links and global CSS displays the dock at **max-width 700px**, including the home page. New qualified organic sessions, tagged partner click counts and APPROVED network payout for this scope: **UNKNOWN**.
5. **Real options and why now.** A status quo: two prominent blue CTAs and secondary link while the fixed dock duplicates category escape; poor first-screen focus. B hide only header button: retains redundant secondary category link and nav dock. **C chosen:** at widths <=700px **only**, remove header CTA and secondary hero text link from visual layout; keep one blue home primary Starta Kostnadskollen, the four purpose choices, visible five-category dock, complete five photo tiles below and all direct category URL destinations. Above 700px everything unchanged. This is a focused follow-up to the 13 Oct screenshot review, not a replacement for P0I–P0M.
6. **Falsifiable success criteria.** At 360/390/430/700px real screenshots: exactly one filled-blue CTA in initial header+hero area, hero H1/body/photo readable and uncut, five direct category links visible in dock, and first primary click reaches `/app/`. At 760/1440px header and secondary links still visible. Any missing direct category, hidden primary, unusually empty header, overlapping photo/text, mobile overflow or poorer visual editorial balance is a **rollback signal**; no presumed CRO lift.
7. **Truth/price constraint.** No price, campaign, claimed savings, order, logo, supplier coverage, ranking or quotation changes. Honest warnings and existing explicit sponsor/partner labeling unchanged.
8. **UI hierarchy/mobile support.** One large filled-blue primary starts an actual useful novice decision. Fixed dock remains a separate low-visual-weight category escape, not an extra commercial button. Existing photos, dark navy headline, ivory surface, 44px touch affordances, keyboard focus and reduced motion unchanged; scope media query exactly matches actual dock breakpoint.
9. **Customer/revenue path.** Search/recommendation -> mobile home -> one clear Starta Kostnadskollen (or direct category dock) -> original free tool -> real, context-matched sponsored comparison/provider -> tagged eligible click -> eventually network APPROVED commission. This correction can affect home choice comprehension, **not** the partner's price engine, conversion eligibility or final payout.

Observe qualified organic users and route choices against prior same-page baseline only after sufficient policy-defined sample, with separately verified clickref/EPI/subID and network-approved SEK; without this report **UNKNOWN**.
