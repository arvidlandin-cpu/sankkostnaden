# Masterplan P3C — coherent insurance customer journeys (2026-10-08)

Owner approved the complete Sänk Kostnaden masterplan. Scope: `/forsakring/` and new self-contained `InsuranceMarketGateway` only, independent of mobile /mobil/.

1. **Audience / task:** A low-information consumer seeks new home or pet insurance, wants to confirm travel cover before paying again, or already has a claim requiring help. These are fundamentally different decisions and cannot honestly share one "best insurer" list.
2. **Competitor and authority evidence:** [Konsumenternas' insurance guidance](https://www.konsumenternas.se/forsakringar/) explains why coverage, deductibles, exclusions and conditions matter. [Compricer home insurance](https://www.compricer.se/forsakring/hem/) has its own comparison/offer flow, whereas Sänk Kostnaden links users outward for personal price quotes. [Europeiska ERV](https://www.erv.se/) is a specialized travel insurance provider; [Insurello](https://insurello.se/) helps with claims after loss on terms user must verify. These are observations of distinct service roles, not evidence of conversion superiority.
3. **Current product observation / hypothesis:** The generic InsurancePartnerMatcher starts with a home-vs-pet question, then shows two featured links and keeps the full selection in collapsed details. Travel and claims appear as separate spotlight blocks after partner content. A new upfront segmented journey with immediately visible coverage-specific partners could reduce category confusion and inappropriate clicks. Any increase in qualified conversion is UNKNOWN.
4. **Our capabilities/data:** 9 genuinely active insurance partner records; 5 match home intent (including independent quote service Compricer), 4 pet, 1 travel, 1 claim, with two providers present in home and pet. All external prices must be checked at providers. First-party organic traffic remains too small for causal proof; last ledger data show 0 approved revenue.
5. **Alternatives:** A) retain generic hidden two-way flow and separate promos. B) only enlarge logos or reveal all partners; still conflates home/pet/travel/claims. C) **chosen** rebuild entire category into home / pet navigation, visible provider groups, and distinct journey cards for travel and claim help, with clearly disclosed partner commissions and purpose. Category-specific guides preserved.
6. **Falsifiable test:** No prerequisite quiz to see every active category provider; 9 unique active partners reachable in a role-matched context; external clicks correctly categorized by home/pet/travel/claims; no pretend cross-type ranking; 5 breakpoint overflow/real browser tests. Future commercial uplift is not measured yet.
7. **Truth:** No fictitious premium, price guarantees, cheapness, coverage recommendations or hints to cancel existing insurance automatically. Compare coverage, exclusions, self-risk, limits and actual supplier quotes. The claims-service fee model must be checked; the travel route explicitly starts with existing home/card protection.
8. **UI design rationale:** Two primary familiar categories, distinct travel/claims paths, 48px brand imagery plus legible true names, roles separated (Compricer quote comparison vs insurer), 44px+ CTA, no hidden full roster. Reuse coherent CSS only where applicable; retain exact canonical/title/description and original guide URLs. Number of links may repeat one eligible insurer in home and pet; the unique total is specified to avoid inflated market-coverage claims.
9. **Revenue chain:** Relevant organic query → correct category help → optional provider quote or actual claims service → tracked link with intent/placement and sponsor label → approved transaction where applicable. No price-ranking by payout. User gets value even without click.
10. **Attribution:** `insurance_hub_path` event with category path and static source only; `data-partner`, `data-category`, `data-intent`, `data-placement` and position preserved for every active external link. No insurance/medical/private user info collected in web events. Exclude test traffic from interpretation.
11. **Safety:** Insurance advice remains general educational content with explicit company terms check. No undisclosed insurer independence claim, fabricated expert credential, quote promise or legal conclusion. New component and page replacement easily reversed. Shared code and long-tail SEO pages unchanged. Full CI, accessibility and mobile verification required.
12. **Decision:** Proceed as a bounded category-specific masterplan milestone if all checks pass. Better full task separation than local design patches; partner coverage and user meaning improve objectively, revenue outcomes remain unproven.
13. **Verifier:** Test home=5, pet=4, travel=1, claims=1, 9 unique partners, sponsor/attribution metadata and alphabetical order within category, known routes, canonical and H1, 360/390/430/1024/1440 screenshots + no overflow, build/integrity/export, commercial browser suite, post-merge live smoke. Retire false assumptions with real data.
