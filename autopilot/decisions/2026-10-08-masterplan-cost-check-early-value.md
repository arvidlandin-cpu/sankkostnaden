# Masterplan phase 2: useful answer after the first Kostnadskollen choice

**Date:** 2026-10-08. Owner: approved full product masterplan. **Scope:** existing indexed `/app/` route, its CSS and QA only; other site pages and partner links remain untouched. KPI: meaningful early decisions and ultimately approved revenue per genuinely relevant visitor, NOT questions completed.

1. **User job:** Primary visitor suspects that household bills are too high but doesn't know existing terms/amounts and may care about only one category. Demand for a 4/4 journey before the first useful recommendation is friction without corresponding guaranteed value.
2. **External comparison:** Elskling can guide a known electricity household into real quote flow; Bredbandsval begins with address/offer availability. Unlike these services, ours spans four kinds of bill and has no personal live quotes. First useful output should not require knowing actual prices, but must be labeled provisional.
3. **Observed vs inferred:** Current /app/ shows question tabs and quick-path partner CTA for fit=2, but the full result area says "Slutför alla fyra områden" until 4/4. We do not know from a user study how many visitors exit at this point. Hypothesis: a safe immediate next-step card reduces perceived effort, without pretending to know biggest savings.
4. **Our data:** GSC /app/ 26 Google clicks/48 impressions in baseline, much of the site's Google visits are to this page; GA4 22 overall organic sessions / 11 organic active users, not enough for controlled conversion conclusions. No approved affiliate revenue yet. This is our highest-discovery product route.
5. **Alternatives:** A) preserve 4-step result gate (simple old logic, high potential friction). B) make guesses about whole household after one answer (false personalization). C) **chosen:** show a clearly named provisional decision and one relevant next-step after first informative answer; allow continuing remaining questions or getting full 4/4 output.
6. **Hypothesis/test:** After answering once, visitor sees first relevant action without completing all four categories; verify for fit=1/2. For fit=0, don't fabricate a problem or promote a partner click; show honest no-clear-issue and next question. Real higher retention/partner conversion UNKNOWN until credible first-party sample.
7. **Truth:** Priority is a deterministic indication of uncertainty/cost worth checking, not market price, individual savings, insurance recommendations or a validated top national ranking. Existing exact cost values remain locally in browser; analytics only get category and abstract progress.
8. **UI:** First start point visible in result area with explicit '1 of 4' / preliminary disclosure; dedicated button in question area scrolls to it. User may continue to other categories from first result or immediately visit existing topic path. Preserve full 4/4 scoring, localStorage migration and optional precise monthly costs.
9. **Revenue chain:** /app/ discoverability → action after 1 choice → category help/real partner alternatives → verified affiliate click → approved transaction. Goal is make visitor find genuinely relevant next step, not click pressure. Current quick external partner path for strong signal continues, and new provisional view is visibly not ranked.
10. **Measurement:** One `cost_check_early_result_available` after first completed area with category, has_signal, number of answers; `cost_check_early_result_opened` from explicit button, `cost_check_early_result_continue` from topic path. No raw monetary inputs. Test/QA traffic separate via `qa=1` and source reporting.
11. **Risks/rollback:** Extra panel layout on 360px, duplicate CTAs, appearing too personalized, event consistency, legacy tests. Address via unobtrusive flexible links, direct disclosure, existing full result kept, tests of fit0 vs fit2 and privacy and mobile. Single-route rollback.
12. **Decision:** execute a bounded end-to-end improvement on highly visible /app/ without requiring a fresh "indexation" waiting hold. Not contingent on electricity or condo improvements proving revenue; concurrent independent scopes already merged.
13. **Verifier:** Existing /app/ canonical/H1 remains, core answer flow unchanged and 4/4 still works; source events privacy-safe; 360/390/430 no horizontal overflow; complete commercial regression, build/export/metadata checks and post-merge production check. Do not describe as measured sales uplift.
