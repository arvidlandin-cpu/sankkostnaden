# P0D shared partner, guide and footer coherence (2026-10-10)

## Decision and complete 13-criteria evidence
1. **User task.** A person moves from a premium-blue home/category or family/first-year cost page into actual sponsored partners, existing SEO guides and the global footer. Product identity must stay consistent and partner intent clear; 0–2 useful clicks, not another form wall.
2. **Observed code & screenshots.** On the blue first-year page the downstream `.mobileMatcher` is old muted forest-green, selected buttons yellow/green, captions ~9 px, CTA dark green. `.siteFooter` and guide-card shell remain tinted forest colors. Observed real 390px exported PNG from fully passing PR #107 QA artifact and `styles/global.css` selectors.
3. **Compared alternatives.** (a) Status quo: leaves product visibly broken and goes against the owner’s Oct 10 design decision. (b) Blind globally replace colors in a 68kB CSS file: rejects due to unknown class interactions/brand palettes. (c) Add a final `!important` override wall: rejects as debt/fragile specificity. **Selected:** source-level replacements only in the defined mobile matcher and footer/shared guide blocks, preserving layout and sponsor destinations.
4. **Competitor/reference observation.** The binding 2026-10-10 product design plan documents Lyst visual joy, The Zebra immediate relevance, KAYAK optional accurate depth. These are inspiration, not copied protected design or proven conversion numbers. We keep brand options accessible before completing calculations.
5. **Source truth.** Current merged `styles/design-tokens.css` defines navy `--sk-ink`, bright-blue `--sk-action`, warm-neutral canvas, light-blue choice; `SiteFooter`, `GuidedPartnerMatchers` and `IntentGuide` already emit usable structural roles. No new source of pricing or rankings.
6. **Expected benefit.** Coherent journey, readable support copy, blue meaningful action, pale-blue selection; customer recognition and UX quality improved as **qualitative hypothesis only**.
7. **Commercial mechanism.** Clearer transparent existing active partner options may reduce cognitive friction before a tracked `affiliate_click`; not proven approved revenue uplift. Do not touch tracking URLs, clickref/EPI, partner eligibility/order or `rel='sponsored'`.
8. **Page structure.** Existing headers, icons, sections, disclosure, exact calculatory fields and forms stay; color/contrast/typography only. Keep one visually dominant blue CTA in partner card, selected filters pale blue rather than lime.
9. **SEO/integrity.** No URL, H1, canonical, on-page text, internal href, metadata, robots, sitemap or JavaScript math modifications.
10. **Privacy/accessibility.** Better 12px helper texts in matcher; mobile buttons min 44–48px with blue focus. No added personal analytics or AI inference.
11. **Baseline.** GSC latest 2026-10-06 1,169 total impressions, 35 clicks; only two to non-home/non-app content. GA4 Organic Search 25 sessions to Oct 9, new site claims not statistically tested. Approved network commissions currently **UNKNOWN** (no valid publisher permission).

Full check, integrity, export, 250+ commercial Playwright and 22 WebKit. Revert PR if visual continuity/contrast/partner accessibility regress. Physical iPhone and Cloudflare exact deployed revision separate UNKNOWN.
