# Masterplan P3D — total cost before choosing personal credit

Date: 2026-10-08. User approved comprehensive rebuild. Scope: pre-existing `/ekonomi/jamfor-privatlan/` guide, calculator component/CSS, pure math and QA. No live loan price data, no product-specific rates, no credit solicitation inside the tool, no new page/indexed URL.

1. **User job:** A person looking at two *actual quoted* personal loan schedules or considering a consolidation offer wants to know whether a lower monthly payment is genuinely cheaper, particularly when term differs. A complete comparison needs effective APR and loan conditions; simple cash-flow arithmetic is only one part.
2. **Independent factual background:** [Konsumenternas' cost of consumption loans](https://www.konsumenternas.se/lan--betalningar/lan/konsumtionslan/kostnader-for-konsumtionslan/) explains checking effective annual interest, all fees, term and total cost. [Konsumenternas loan calculator](https://www.konsumenternas.se/konsumentstod/kalkyler--verktyg/lanekalkyl/) already provides a full loan schedule and interest/repayment modelling. [Konsumentverket consumer credit](https://www.konsumentverket.se/ekonomi/lana-pengar/) explains real effective cost and fees. Our utility **does not duplicate their lender-grade amortization/APR tool**: it illustrates a narrower question with payment figures already in an actual offer.
3. **Observed site gap and inference:** `/ekonomi/jamfor-privatlan/` currently explains effective rate, terms, fees and total repayment in text, but gives no immediate comparison using two schedules. Hypothesis: an illustrative two-column calculator helps novices see why a lower monthly figure can cost more overall. It is not measured consumer retention or sales proof.
4. **Our evidence:** 3 currently active loan-intent affiliate programs and no verified live individual rates; GSC organic traffic still very low with zero approved commissions at last reporting. The downstream loan matcher uses static intent priority and cannot show verified individualized "best loan".
5. **Alternatives:** A) text-only educational guide (lowest complexity but little immediate hands-on utility). B) invent an interest-rate calculator and APR/offer estimates (higher compliance/data risk; duplicates a better official tool). C) **chosen:** compute the total sum from consumer-entered quoted installment, months and separately billed mandatory fees for each of two offers **for the same principal**; highlight lower-monthly versus lower-total conflict; link directly to the authoritative full amortization/APR tool.
6. **Falsifiable hypothesis:** With illustrative 100,000 kr principal, A (2300+25)*60+200 = 139,700 kr, B (1650+25)*96+200 = 161,000 kr: lower monthly B is 21,300 kr costlier in total. Browser checks validate this and invalid-underprincipal cases; organic conversion effect remains UNKNOWN.
7. **Offer/source truth:** All default figures conspicuously labeled ILLUSTRATIVE, not real credit offers, advertisements, rates or APR. User may insert their own payment amounts. The component explicitly does not calculate true effective interest, future variable-rate changes, balloon/residual or amortization schedules; flags totals below principal as invalid. Original lender SEKKI terms determine obligation. No recommendation to take or increase credit.
8. **Hierarchy/mobile:** Inline right after the existing guide checklist; one shared principal and two labeled offer groups, clear full-term totals and warning when cheaper-monthly is higher-total; official source link. Two desktop columns; one column at 360/390/430. Keyboard accessible inputs and aria-live result. No needless unrelated affiliate links in the tool.
9. **Commercial chain:** Relevant search may receive an original, linkable decision aid; user can then compare real lender offers *only if appropriate*. On-page generic approved loan partners remain separate and truthful. Never conflate fewer clicks with worse user service or push credit unnecessarily.
10. **Analytics:** One `loan_total_comparison_used` event on first deliberate input edit, containing static source/mode only. No principal, installment, fee or loan duration sent. Existing context/pageview and partner click tracking unchanged.
11. **Privacy, trust, compliance, rollback:** No account data collected, no quote transmitted, no lender rate or success guarantee. Any promotional credit legal notice already required elsewhere remains in place; content is educational and should be reviewed for applicable marketing requirements before new promotional claims. Existing canonical/H1 and long-tail SEO preserved. Component can be independently removed.
12. **Decision:** Implement as an original, conservative utility on an already indexed comparison guide, not new keyword filler. Value is easier recognition of financing cost even without approved affiliate click. Do not declare conversion improvement before data.
13. **Verifier:** Build, static export and internal link checker; browser math 139,700/161,000 difference 21,300; changed duration yields 100,700 B total; invalid insufficient repayment blocks a misleading result; official source link; privacy-safe analytics; 360/390/430/1024 no horizontal scroll; commercial regression and post-main live checks. If any calculation misleads users, roll back.
