# Masterplan P3A — broadband as a real address-based decision (2026-10-08)

**Scope:** /bredband/ category only; approved masterplan phase P3, no new affiliate partner or paid service. Compared to the independently shipped /elavtal/ route and broader P2 /app/ entry path. Reuses validated layout style but follows a category-specific consumer job.

1. **Audience / task:** A Swedish consumer who suspects they overpay for broadband wants to know if their address supports the offer, which speed is sufficient, whether cheaper promotional months offset higher regular prices, and how to move to an available provider. They need not know their street address to browse this site.
2. **Competitor evidence:** Bredbandsval https://www.bredbandsval.se/ shows an address-based offer finder; the site's existing approved affiliate program explicitly notes availability and price are checked there. Swedish ISP providers Ownit https://www.ownit.se/ and Internetport https://www.internetport.se/ allow customer-level availability confirmation. These can possess real offer/address data we DO NOT have. This is product flow observation, not an independently audited conversion result.
3. **Observed issue / mechanism:** Existing `CategoryLanding` has an intro checklist, after which `BroadbandPartnerMatcher` shows 2 direct partner cards and asks two questions (access / compare-or-direct), then 3 suggested partners and a collapsed directory; the same comparison service can appear multiple times. Some mobile broadband referrals originate from partners in the mobile category and were relabeled as broadband: not inherently wrong but conflates categories for a novice. Hypothesis: a single primary address-checking choice and visible simple options prevent false confidence and lessen friction. This is not yet a proven performance effect.
4. **Our measurement:** Organic site still very small (30-day GSC 35 total clicks, 22 organic GA4 sessions) and 0 approved affiliate revenue. There are 3 approved, active broadband-category programs in code (Bredbandsval.se, Ownit, Internetport); no whole-market coverage and no live address-price feed.
5. **Three choices:** (A) status quo: multiple repeated intermediate steps. (B) only increase logos/open directory: doesn't explain the address eligibility dependency. (C) **chosen**: rebuild route around real comparison-service address check, accessible no-login speed-selection help, 2 direct operators alphabetically, and a link to the existing exact first-year total calculator. This makes the prominent action more precise and avoids false local live data.
6. **Specific falsifiable hypothesis:** New page exposes one direct path to real address-based offers and both verified providers with NO prerequisite questions, while preserving an obvious speed helper and 12-month cost comparison. This can be confirmed immediately by browser tests. Qualified referral traffic / approved affiliate revenue uplift cannot yet be inferred.
7. **Offer truth:** At address check we explicitly say user's address is entered at the comparison provider, and no address data is sent to Sänk Kostnaden on this page. Individual providers require address-specific eligibility confirmation. There is no claim we deliver personalized broadband prices, have all operators, know true "cheapest", or have verified promotional amounts.
8. **UI:** Original targeted broadband hero, primary address-availability CTA, one separate help/speed route, clearly visible names and roles of all three approved partners, large brand text/icons and 48px-plus buttons, annual calculator at bottom. Screen sizes 360/390/430/1024/1440, direct keyboard and tap navigation, dated explicit sponsor disclosure.
9. **Revenue route:** Appropriate organic intent → answer utility / trustworthy address eligibility → tracked Bredbandsval/ISP click → real provider/partner transaction → approved commission. Explicit `broadband_hub_path` events observe decision choices without transmitting raw address or cost.
10. **Measurement plan:** QA and test traffic excluded where supported; referral click events retain `data-partner`, `data-category=bredband`, intent and placement. New hero and direct links must preserve known tracking URLs. No traffic target claims without real sample; review downstream approved results as signals mature.
11. **SEO, safety, rollback:** Exact existing canonical, title, description, breadcrumbs retained; established useful guide URLs and more guides included. No new paid integrations, no mass SEO pages, no fake prices, no cross-category unverified redirects. The page-only rewrite plus new component and CSS reuse can be reverted cleanly. Be mindful that the existing generic inline cost calculator is replaced by an explicit link to the fully tested broadband calculator; this is an intentional simplification, measurable in user behavior.
12. **Decision:** implement because address eligibility is fundamentally more important than premature choice of an operator; a distinct single-screen journey with visible coverage, speed-help and annual cost better fits consumer task. Do not ship just to look busy: all build and commercial QA must be green.
13. **Verification:** Browser test asserts Bredbandsval and two distinct providers / exact sponsor tracking attributes; sorted names, correct canonical and real speed/annual calculator href, no gating. Real responsive test at 5 breakpoints; build, static export, SEO checker, commercial regression and production smoke after merge. No winner or financial claim until confirmed real attribution / organic cohorts.
