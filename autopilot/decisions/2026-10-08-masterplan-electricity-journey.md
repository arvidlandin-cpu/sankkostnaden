# Masterplan 1A: Electricity category is a decision journey, not a hidden affiliate widget
**Date:** 2026-10-08. **Status:** implementation under QA; decision: bounded whole-category experience redesign. **Scope:** /elavtal/ only. **Owner authorization:** "kör masterplan" after approving 2026-10-08 full-site masterplan. No user account, no paid service, no new partner contract.

1. **Audience/job:** Novice searching "jämför elavtal" wants to understand whether to compare several electric providers, visit an existing provider, or learn the pricing basis. Existing users can use precise price inputs. The first useful next step must not depend on four questions or an unexplained ranking.

2. **Competitor observation (research basis):** [Elskling](https://www.elskling.se/) publicly guides visitors toward actual electricity quotes based on household information. [Elpriskollen](https://elpriskollen.se/) uses actual public electric comparison data. Both have data capabilities our static affiliate site does not. See `COMPETITOR_INTELLIGENCE.md` (first sweep 2026-10-08). No claim their layout has a measured conversion rate.

3. **Observation vs. hypothesis:** Source audit of `CategoryLanding.tsx` → `PartnerDirectory.tsx` → `ElectricityPartnerMatcher`: two immediate partner links; an additional 3-choice quiz; 3 dynamically ranked partner cards; a hidden-by-default 12-partner directory; tiny generic favicons and duplicate explanatory text. **Hypothesis:** separating true multi-offer comparison from direct electric suppliers, exposing real brands without quiz dependency and linking accurately to a full cost tool makes choices clearer. Conversion lift **UNKNOWN**.

4. **Our evidence:** 2026-10-08 30-day GSC: 1,265 impressions / 35 clicks overall, query "jämför elavtal" 61 impressions / 0 clicks at avg 24.6, `/elavtal/jamfor-elavtal/` 102 impressions / 0 clicks, category organic data too sparse for causal analysis. 12 active actual electric partner links in `lib/partners.ts`, including comparison service Elskling and 11 suppliers. No live quote feed; latest approved affiliate transactions = 0. Owner screenshot shows hidden partners and competing components. No baseline appropriate to promise financial uplift.

5. **Alternatives:** A) Keep the old design (no rework, but three overlapping layers). B) Enlarge logos / open the directory (doesn't solve unclear semantics). C) **Chosen:** simplify the entire category page into two explicit distinct commercial routes, show all 11 companies in alphabetical brand directory, provide one optional knowledge path and one precise cost tool, maintain category-specific SEO and guide links. More substantial than patchwork, still limited to one category.

6. **Falsifiable hypothesis:** The live layout removes at least one mandatory interaction to reach every active provider and resolves the first-screen role ambiguity. Can verify immediately: all 12 partners present and reachable with sponsored links, none gated. Future qualified organic partner-click propensity and approved affiliate commission **unknown pending real audience sample**; monitor `electricity_hub_path` (intent only), existing affiliate-click tracking and approved conversions.

7. **Truth/offer sourcing:** `getActivePartners('el',undefined,40)` loads currently active tracked links. Elskling is identified by verified explicit partner name as *jämförelsetjänst*, the other 11 as *enskilt elbolag*. No claiming market-wide coverage, cheapest supplier, price comparison in-house, guaranteed savings or user-specific recommendation. Offers verified only on partner sites; direct suppliers alphabetically sorted, never monetization score. Small brand favicons already used by site are decorative; exact brand names shown in prominent readable typography, not claims of official partnership endorsements beyond actual affiliates.

8. **Mobile and interaction hierarchy:** Category hero one primary "Se aktiva elalternativ", one assistance path to contract-type guide; comparison card and direct-supplier card with 54px CTAs, 48px identifiable brand logos; real provider list always visible. Link to existing exact annual-cost tool. No extra quiz choice or collapsed 12-partner roster. 360/390/430/1024/1440px Playwright checks, full-page screenshots, keyboard focus and target sizes; accessible headings and link names.

9. **Revenue chain:** Search discovery → category decision → existing tracked partner link tagged with data-category/intent/placement → partner-side user action → approved revenue. Other legitimate route goes to cost calculator before returning. Display order not commission-led; target is higher *relevant* visit value. We cannot attribute direct commission changes to a single change until a sufficient qualified cohort is available.

10. **Attribution, sample and review:** `electricity_hub_path` records 4 explicit route types with source only, no consumption/pricing information. Every partner click retains sponsored/nofollow and `data-partner`, category, placement and position; existing `AffiliateTracking` handles actual link IDs. Prefer visits from organic/referral post-instrumentation, exclude QA and owner traffic. Observe data weekly and revisit only after meaningful distinct users and correct downstream tracking, not an arbitrary 15-Oct lock. No fake experiment winner/loser.

11. **Risk and rollback:** Shared components and other categories unchanged. Preserve canonical, title, descriptions, guide URLs and current active partner tracking URLs. This page-only replacement is reversible in one PR. Potential loss of existing detailed cost-calculation inline component mitigated by visible link to existing tested annual calculator. CI and production smoke plus manual mobile accessibility inspection are required before declaring complete.

12. **Decision:** **EXECUTE** because structurally conflicting route layers are independently visible in current source and user feedback, and page-rewrite is a better response than logo-only patches. No expense. All affiliate/sponsor disclosures kept. Existing active experiment state = no growth experiments. Implementation is not reported as commercially "winning" by virtue of shipping.

13. **Verification:** Confirm 1 comparison-service active link, 11 active direct suppliers alphabetical, no hidden roster, display counts equal actual source, real tracking attrs, canonical, focusable buttons/links, screenshot and no overflow at all mobile + desktop breakpoints, build/export/integrity, complete commercial regression. Post-merge verify Cloudflare deployment route and metadata when possible. Log approved revenue changes only when attribution is confirmed. Later rollout to other categories must be category-specific and separately reviewed.
