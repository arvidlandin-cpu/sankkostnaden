# Masterplan P3B — Mobile decisions, real partner visibility and family total cost

Date: 2026-10-08. Owner approved implementation of full masterplan. One independent scope: `/mobil/`, its related partner-role metadata and mobile-specific test. Reuse already tested layout classes only; do not automatically copy electricity editorial decisions.

1. **Who / job:** A low-information Swedish visitor wants a reasonable mobile plan, but may not know current spend or usage. They can be shopping for one plan or for an entire household. Their primary constraint could be coverage, consumption or real recurring household cost.

2. **What actual competitors show (dated public primary sites):** [Tele2 family](https://www.tele2.se/mobilabonnemang/familj) visibly offers a main plan with additional members, their own surf allowances, and quoted binding terms. [Hallon family](https://www.hallon.se/mobilabonnemang/familjeabonnemang) visibly describes additional family users sharing data and distinguishes introductory prices from prices afterwards. [Comviq family](https://www.comviq.se/mobilabonnemang/familj?type=familjeabonnemang) publicly shows separate data amounts per member and main/extra prices. These **observed commercial offers** are not evidence that their funnels convert better than ours; terms/campaigns may change.

3. **Observed deficiency versus hypothesis:** Existing `MobilePartnerMatcher.tsx` shows a two-part quiz, then an unexplained static priority short list of three, with all ten partners in collapsed details and micro-brand imagery. A commercial direct strip precedes the quiz, but repeats some options. This creates avoidable steps and can imply personalized operator suitability without a validated network/price feed. Hypothesis: clear task routing, prominent full operator visibility, and total cost context increase informed progression without arbitrary partner ranking. This effect remains UNKNOWN.

4. **Own evidence:** Mobile category contains 10 active approved general mobile programs in source; separate related refurbished partner is not active and must not be promoted as a verified purchase path. First-party organic traffic remains extremely sparse (GSC 35 Google clicks overall / 30 days, GA4 22 organic sessions), so no valid conversion uplift predictions. Underlying family calculator `/mobil/lonar-sig-familjeabonnemang/` already computes 12-month totals for two to five members.

5. **Alternatives:** A) Keep current two-question matcher and hidden partners: lowest effort, high friction / weaker discoverability. B) Enlarge logos and open details: better surface but no task clarity or economic differentiation. C) **Chosen** coherent mobile landing: 2 clear tasks (single-user surf guidance or family cost calculation), full alphabetically sorted active operator directory, first-year family calculator handoff, coverage/conditions checklist and retained guides. No operator order by commission.

6. **Falsifiable immediate value:** Every active mobile operator reachable from one visible page without completing questions; no inactive refurbished partner affiliate CTA; correct SEO title/canonical, ten actual active operator links, visible appropriate tools and honest family caveats. Future hypothesis: increased qualified partner-handoff/approved revenue per relevant visitor; requires real organic sample.

7. **Offer truth and freshness:** Never show unverified prices, discounts, live national cheapest, speed coverage, data limits or fake review scores. Mark promotional details as requiring confirmation at provider. Explicitly distinguish **shared** vs **individual** family data allowances; this difference is grounded in Tele2/Hallon primary sources. Hallon was missing family-intent metadata despite offering family sharing: corrected with official first-party documentation. No claim all family-tagged offers are suitable for every family.

8. **Front-end hierarchy:** Hero gives user first two plain-language choices. Single-plan guide /mobil/hur-mycket-surf-behover-jag; family original math /mobil/lonar-sig-familjeabonnemang. All operators immediately visible with 48px brand icons plus real bold names (favicon decorative, no endorsement), 44px+ CTAs, clear sponsored disclosure. No redundant matcher or collapsed directory. 360/390/430/1024/1440 breakpoints and accessible keyboard targets.

9. **Revenue model:** Qualified discovery -> human-meaningful route -> direct partner if appropriate -> actual provider pricing/onboarding -> tracked tagged clickRef/EPI -> approved commission. Preserve all current verified outbound tracking URLs, partner category and position metadata. No fabricated commercial performance or payout-based ranking.

10. **Instrumentation:** `mobile_hub_path` only reports path identifier (single_surf_help, family_calculator, direct_operator, family_total_cost, network_guide) and source. Existing partner tracking captures referral click placements (`mobile_hub_operator`) and partner identity. No phone numbers, bills or costs in events. Organic visitor cohort required before ROI judgments.

11. **Risk/rollback:** New code only for `/mobil/` plus a self-contained component. Shared visual CSS from already shipped electricity; existing family calculation and SEO-targeted long-tail guides unchanged. Explicitly do not display closed refurbished program. Brand assets are generic favicons; any official logo replacements need usage permission review. Roll back page and component if tracked links, route, mobile a11y, integrity or production smoke fail.

12. **Decision:** SHIP if CI is green: fundamental consumer-task architecture beats piecemeal logo patches while small traffic volume calls for a discoverable genuinely useful entry. Zero paid spend and no contracts. Improvement is objectively inspectable but cannot be declared a commercial win.

13. **Verifier:** static integrity and export, full commercial regression with all 10 live-category partner link attributes, consistent sorting, no broken canonical/guide links, 360/390/430/1024/1440 no horizontal overflow plus screenshots, no dormant-refurbished outbound CTA, post-main build and production smoke. Review correct tracking and eventual approved revenue when actual eligible users arrive, not on arbitrary SEO embargo.
