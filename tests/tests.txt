# Tests

## Test 1 - Build a personalized cost priority [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: interactive diagnostic, live scoring, category ranking, direct comparison routing
Description: Verifies that household answers change the diagnostic and produce an actionable ranked result.

Steps:
1. Open Kostnadskollen and choose El
2. Enter 1500 kr, choose 2+ år / aldrig, Osäker and Kan flytta förbrukning
3. Review the personal cost map and select Kontrollera nu for the highest ranked category

Expected: El receives a visibly elevated score, appears at or near the top of the personal ranking, and its action opens the electricity comparison journey.

## Test 5 - Smart selectors create actionable need profiles
Viewport: mobile
Covers: broadband speed selector, free-surf value test, fiber-vs-5G selector, deeper electricity contract selector, insurance protection check, direct comparison routing
Description: Verifies the search-oriented smart tools turn household answers into useful profiles without unsupported market-price or savings claims.

Steps:
1. Open the free-surf test, choose high data use, frequent streaming, regular hotspot, little wifi and maximum freedom; confirm a high-utility result
2. Open the fiber-vs-5G test, choose several simultaneous users, high stability needs and weak mobile signal; confirm a fiber-oriented result and comparison action
3. Open the electricity contract test, choose steerable loads and active/automatic control; confirm a dynamic-price-oriented profile and relevant next action

Expected: Each tool produces a clearly differentiated result from the selected answers, explains the decision factors, and routes to a relevant comparison or deeper guide without promising a guaranteed saving.
